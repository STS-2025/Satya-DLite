## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a clinical, pure, and dependable digital presence for a premium South Indian edible oil brand. The aesthetic merges FMCG retail credibility with modern corporate restraint.

### Brand Personality & Core Principles
- **Purity and Precision:** Visuals emphasize transparency, lab-tested cleanliness, and uncompromised food quality.
- **Rooted Modernity:** A contemporary presentation that honors regional culinary heritage without leaning on rustic tropes or decorative clutter.
- **Quiet Authority:** Premium status is communicated through disciplined spacing, exact alignment, and subtle tonal depth rather than ostentatious embellishments.

### Design Style
The system employs a refined Corporate Modern minimalism. It relies on a rigorous grayscale baseline, pristine white surfaces, and an uncompromising corporate blue signature. Visual complexity is minimized to keep attention focused on product specifications, nutritional integrity, and quality certifications.

## Colors

The palette enforces a disciplined hierarchy designed around crisp retail clarity and clinical confidence.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#145DA0`):** Satya D'Lite Blue. Reserved for primary actions, verification marks, active focal states, and core branding elements.
- **Secondary (`#0B2545`):** Deep Navy. Used for authoritative structural blocks, high-contrast footers, table headers, and dominant display typography.
- **Tertiary (`#4A6B82`):** Muted Slate Blue. Serves as a supporting bridge for secondary metadata, interactive outlines, and active filter tags.
- **Neutral Core (`#1E252B`):** Deep Charcoal. Provides maximum contrast and legibility for body text, avoiding the harshness of pure black while maintaining clinical clarity.

### Functional Roles & Canvas Foundation
- **Canvas Base:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) forms the foundational surface to emulate lab-level purity.
- **Surface Neutrals:** Crisp cool grays (`#F6F8FA`, `#ECEFF2`) provide low-contrast zoning for cards, tables, and product spec sheets.
- **Dividers & Borders:** Subtle Cool Slate (`#D8DEE4`) anchors component boundaries with strict, 1px rules.

## Typography

The typographic pairing pairs `Plus Jakarta Sans` for modern structural headers with `Noto Sans` for body copy, offering natural compatibility with vernacular scripts such as Noto Sans Tamil.

### Scale Application
- **Display & Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, geometric letterforms set with tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`). Delivers an immediate impression of industrial consistency and contemporary packaging precision.
- **Body Text (`Noto Sans`):** High legibility at small sizes, optimal x-height, and dependable multi-lingual glyph rendering for regional compliance, nutritional tables, and ingredient decks.
- **Labels & Badges (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Medium to bold weights with uppercase tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) reserved for certifications, FSSAI indicators, volume sizes, and technical attributes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is founded on a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display structured retail specifications alongside expansive brand storytelling.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, `3rem` (`48px`) outer margins. Maximum layout container width is fixed at `1280px` for optimal density.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `2rem` (`32px`) outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `1.25rem` (`20px`) outer margins.

### Spacing Cadence
- Content elements align along an explicit 8-point base rhythm.
- Spacing inside data cells and nutrition components relies on `space-xs` and `space-sm` to maintain analytical precision.
- Section-to-section flow relies on generous vertical padding (`space-xl` scaled up to `4rem` on desktop) to evoke openness and high-tier product confidence.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids theatrical drop shadows in favor of understated, calibrated layers that convey clinical purity and architectural stability.

### The Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Flat Baseline (`level-0`):** Surface panels, table cells, and standard layout containers reside directly on the canvas without elevation, bounded by `1px solid #D8DEE4`.
2. **Subtle Float (`level-1`):** Applied to product cards, SKU selection containers, and interactive search toolbars. Composed of an ultra-diffused, ambient shadow:
   `0 1px 3px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.03)`.
3. **Interactive Lift (`level-2`):** Triggered strictly upon mouse hover on interactive cards and quick-view panels. The shadow expands slightly while staying crisp:
   `0 4px 8px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.06), 0 12px 24px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.05)`.
4. **Modal & Navigation Shell (`level-3`):** Fixed navigation ribbons, flyout shopping carts, and nutritional verification overlays use:
   `0 8px 16px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08), 0 24px 48px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.06)`.

Depth is prioritized through high tonal contrast (navy over pure white) and clean outlines rather than heavy blur planes.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a restrained `roundedness: 1` shape language across all digital touchpoints.

### Corner Radius Implementation
- **Base Components (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Inputs, buttons, nutritional badges, drop-down selects, and SKU selection chips utilize a disciplined, subtle corner rounding. This preserves geometric precision while avoiding brittle, needle-sharp edges.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / `8px`):** Product summary cards, laboratory analysis accordions, and hero banners.
- **Modals & Overlays (`rounded-xl` / `0.75rem` / `12px`):** Sticky product drawers, confirmation sheets, and filter dialogs.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Pure pill geometry (`rounded-full`) is strictly reserved for verification checkmarks, batch status dots, and standard micro status tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#145DA0`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `0.25rem`. Hover state shifts tone to `#0B2545` with a seamless `150ms` ease-in-out transition. Active state applies `transform: scale(0.99)`.
- **Secondary (Outline):** 1px border `#145DA0`, text `#145DA0`, background transparent. Hover initiates a gentle background wash of `rgba(20, 93, 160, 0.04)`.
- **Tertiary (Ghost):** Minimal padding, text `#0B2545`, weight `600`. Hover presents text underline with a subtle slate tint (`#4A6B82`).

### Product & Nutrition Cards
- **Construction:** Crisp white canvas (`#FFFFFF`) with a continuous border (`1px solid #D8DEE4`) and `0.25rem` corners.
- **Visual Staging:** Top area features an unadorned, crisp product cutout over a `#F6F8FA` neutral pedestal. The lower section includes bold SKU metadata, volume metrics, and retail pricing.
- **Hover Behavior:** Translates upward by `-2px`, shifting from `level-1` to `level-2` elevation with zero border color shifts.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default State:** `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border `#D8DEE4`, text `#1E252B`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Selected State:** `#0B2545` fill, border `#0B2545`, text `#FFFFFF`. 
- **Application:** Used for pack sizes (e.g., "500ml", "1L Pouch", "5L Can"), extraction types ("Cold Pressed", "Refined"), and purity criteria.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Height `44px`, 1px border `#D8DEE4`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E252B`, border-radius `0.25rem`. Focus applies an exterior 2px ring in `rgba(20, 93, 160, 0.2)` and turns the border `#145DA0`.
- **Labels:** Set in `label-sm`, positioned statically above input containers with a `0.375rem` gap for immediate legibility.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkbox:** `18px x 18px` square with `2px` border-radius. Inactive border `#D8DEE4`. Checked state fills `#145DA0` featuring a white checkmark icon.
- **Radio Button:** Standard circular form factor. Active state displays an interior `#145DA0` focal pip surrounded by an active ring.

### FMCG Quality Badges & Accordions
- **Purity Accordion:** Collapsible technical specifications detailing smoke point, iodine value, fatty acid profiles, and certified source origin. Features strict `1px solid #D8DEE4` row dividers and fluid expand-collapse iconography.
- **Lab-Certified Seal:** Compact layout with deep navy typography (`#0B2545`), micro-check iconography in `#145DA0`, and subtle gray background pills (`#ECEFF2`) to guarantee consumer trust.
