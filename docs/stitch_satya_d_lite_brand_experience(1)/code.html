About Satya D'Lite & Dayan Enterprises

Honest Milling, Wholesome Care, and Sacred Devotion

தூய மரபு, அன்றாட நலம் — நல்வாழ்வின் அடித்தளம்.

Manufactured by Dayan Enterprises & Co. in West Thoothukudi, Tamil Nadu, Satya D'Lite represents an enduring dedication to genuine expeller pressing. We screen and mill clean, sun-dried copra to deliver wholesome edible coconut oils for cooking and daily hair nourishment, alongside sanctified pancha deepam oils for traditional temple and home altars.

By pairing traditional expeller pressing with modern hygiene standards, zero chemical refining, and tamper-proof packaging, we supply consistent, reliable quality to families, temples, and institutional partners across Southern India.
